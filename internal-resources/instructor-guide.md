  <span class="headline">CVEs</span>
  <span class="subhead">Instructor Guide</span>
</h1>

This module aims to foster awareness of CVEs and drive home the importance of timely software updates and managing tech debt. Be sure to keep the lecture conversational and use real-world examples from your experience to help students realize the relevance of this concept.

Also, be sure to have the [online CVE resource](https://cve.mitre.org/) readily available to show students where to learn more about them.
